OUR HERITAGE & CRAFTSMANSHIP

Threads of Tradition: The Art of Ilkal Weaving

Ilkal is a town in Bagalkot district, Karnataka, renowned for its rich handloom heritage. Ilkal sarees are celebrated for their distinctive borders, vibrant traditional colours and unique weaving patterns that have been cherished across generations.

One of the most distinctive features of an Ilkal saree is the traditional Topi Tene weaving technique. In this method, the pallu is woven separately and attached to the body of the saree by interlocking a series of loops with the warp. This traditional craftsmanship gives Ilkal sarees their unique identity.

Traditionally woven on pit looms in North Karnataka, particularly in the Bagalkot district, Ilkal sarees are crafted using different yarns: cotton and viscose for the body, and pure silk for the pallu.

Behind every saree is the dedication, patience and skill of traditional weavers who have preserved this beautiful art across generations.

At RAGA ILKAL SAREES, we celebrate this heritage and bring the beauty of traditional Ilkal sarees closer to women who appreciate culture, craftsmanship and timeless elegance.
