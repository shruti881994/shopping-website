Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–3 business days, excluding Sundays and public holidays.

  • Processing begins after the order is successfully confirmed and payment is verified, where applicable.
  • Dispatch and tracking information will be shared through WhatsApp or another available communication channel, where tracking is supported.

Shipping Methods & Delivery Timelines

We arrange domestic shipping through available courier services to bring your sarees safely to you.

  • Domestic shipping: Generally 4–7 business days after dispatch.
  • Delivery timelines may vary depending on destination and courier availability.
  • Festive seasons, public holidays, sale periods and unforeseen courier delays may affect delivery timelines.

RAGA ILKAL SAREES is based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, and accepts orders across India, subject to courier service availability at the destination PIN code.

Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges, if any, will be communicated or displayed before your order is confirmed. Free-shipping offers are subject to the eligibility conditions and terms announced with each offer.

Address Accuracy

Please provide complete and accurate delivery details when placing your order.

  • Full name and mobile number.
  • House or flat number and street address.
  • Area, city, state and PIN code.

Incorrect or incomplete details may cause delays or unsuccessful deliveries. Please contact us as soon as possible if you notice an error.

Undelivered or Returned Packages

If a parcel cannot be delivered because of an incorrect address, unsuccessful delivery attempts or refusal, please contact us to discuss the available next steps. Additional shipping charges may apply if the parcel needs to be shipped again.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, we will share available tracking details through WhatsApp or another available communication channel. Use the tracking link or consignment number provided by the courier to follow your shipment.
